Analyse du programme

The Textile Society of America invites abstracts for an edited anthology titled 'One Stitch at a Time,' exploring embroidery and needlecraft as narrative technologies for storytelling, cultural memory, and resistance. Contributors submit a 150-word abstract with 3 keywords and 50-word bio by December 10, 2026; accepted contributors then submit a completed essay of minimum 7,000 words by June 30, 2027. The anthology will be published by a university press in the United States.

Pour qui : Scholars, artists, writers, and researchers in embroidery, textile arts, needlecraft, and related disciplines exploring themes of cultural identity, resistance, activism, human rights, social memory, and narrative within literature, visual arts, history, and cultural studies.

À savoir
  • No monetary compensation—this is a publication opportunity, not a funded fellowship, residency, or grant
  • Two distinct deadlines: abstract submission December 10, 2026, and completed essay submission June 30, 2027 (only if abstract accepted)
  • Substantial written output required: minimum 7,000-word essay for accepted contributors
  • Publication through academic university press; visibility primarily within scholarly and institutional circles rather than broad public distribution
  • Submission process is email-based; no online submission platform
  • Acceptance is competitive; abstract submission does not guarantee inclusion
